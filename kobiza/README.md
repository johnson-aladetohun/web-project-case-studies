# Kobiza

Multi-tenant commerce and business-management SaaS for African merchants.

## Work represented
- Laravel backend with React/TypeScript frontend
- Product catalogue, POS, inventory, invoicing and payments
- Multi-tenant business workspaces and staff permissions
- Reporting, procurement, CRM, loyalty and marketing workflows
- Automated tests and CI-oriented engineering practices
